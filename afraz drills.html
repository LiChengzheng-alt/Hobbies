Dribbling & Ball Control

Cone Weave (Beginner)

Duration: 10 minutes | Equipment: 6-8 cones or markers

  1. Set up 6-8 cones in a straight line, about 1 metre apart.
  2. Dribble the ball through the cones using only the inside and outside of one foot, keeping the ball close.
  3. Turn at the end and weave back using your other foot.
  4. Repeat for 5 rounds, gradually increasing your speed as your control improves.

Tip: Keep your head up between touches so you get used to scanning the pitch while dribbling.

1v1 Box Isolation (Intermediate)

Duration: 15 minutes | Equipment: 4 cones, 1 partner

  1. Mark out a 5x5 metre box with cones.
  2. One player attacks with the ball, the other defends inside the box.
  3. The attacker tries to dribble past the defender and out the far side; the defender tries to win the ball or push the attacker out of bounds.
  4. Swap roles after each attempt and complete 10 reps each.

Tip: Use quick changes of direction and a low centre of gravity to unbalance the defender before accelerating past them.

Cut-Inside Finish (Advanced)

Duration: 15 minutes | Equipment: 3 cones, a goal or target

  1. Start near the touchline with the ball at your feet, facing the goal at an angle.
  2. Dribble toward a cone placed to represent a defender.
  3. Use a sharp inside-foot touch to cut infield past the cone, then immediately curl a shot toward the far corner.
  4. Complete 10 reps from the right side, then 10 from the left to develop both feet.

Tip: This is the exact wing-to-shot pattern that makes wide forwards like Lamine Yamal so dangerous - the touch and the shot should happen almost in one motion.

Passing & Vision

Wall Pass Accuracy (Beginner)

Duration: 10 minutes | Equipment: A flat wall, 2 cones

  1. Place two cones on the wall about 1 metre apart to mark a target zone.
  2. Stand 5-8 metres back and pass the ball firmly into the target zone.
  3. Control the rebound with one touch and pass again.
  4. Complete 20 passes with your dominant foot, then 20 with your weaker foot.

Tip: Strike through the middle of the ball with the inside of your foot for a clean, accurate pass.

Rondo (Piggy in the Middle) (Intermediate)

Duration: 15 minutes | Equipment: Cones, 5+ players

  1. Mark out a circle or square roughly 8x8 metres.
  2. Four or more players pass the ball around the outside while one or two defenders try to intercept it in the middle.
  3. Limit outside players to 1-2 touches to force quick decision making.
  4. Any player who loses the ball or gives a bad pass swaps into the middle.

Tip: Scan the pitch before the ball arrives so you already know your next pass.

Third Man Runs (Advanced)

Duration: 20 minutes | Equipment: Cones, 3+ players

  1. Set up three players in a triangle roughly 15 metres apart.
  2. Player A passes to Player B, who lays the ball off first-time.
  3. As soon as A passes, Player C (the "third man") sprints into open space to receive the lay-off and drive forward.
  4. Rotate positions after every 5 reps so everyone practices the run, the pass, and the lay-off.

Tip: The timing of the run matters more than the speed - leave too early and you'll be caught offside or unmarked too soon.

Shooting & Finishing

Target Zone Shooting (Beginner)

Duration: 10 minutes | Equipment: A goal, cones to mark corners

  1. Use cones to section off the two top corners of the goal as target zones.
  2. Place the ball 12-16 metres out and take 10 shots aiming for the target zones.
  3. Focus on a smooth run-up and planting your standing foot beside the ball.
  4. Track how many shots land in the target zones and try to beat your score next session.

Tip: Lean slightly over the ball on your strike to keep your shot low and on target.

First-Touch Finish (Intermediate)

Duration: 15 minutes | Equipment: A goal, 1 partner or rebounder

  1. Have a partner feed you passes from different angles just outside the box.
  2. Aim to strike the ball first-time toward goal without taking a controlling touch.
  3. Alternate between passes to your left and right side to practice finishing across your body.
  4. Complete 15 reps, resting between sets.

Tip: Get your body side-on to the pass early so you're already balanced when the ball arrives.

Striker's Movement Circuit (Advanced)

Duration: 20 minutes | Equipment: A goal, cones, 1 server

  1. Start on the edge of the box with a server holding the ball near the penalty spot.
  2. Make a checking run away from goal, then explode back toward the six-yard box.
  3. The server plays a low cross or through-ball timed to your run for a first-time finish.
  4. Vary your movement each rep: near post run, far post run, and dropping short.

Tip: This is the same central-striker movement Ousmane Dembélé developed when he was repositioned up front for PSG.

Speed & Agility

Ladder Footwork Basics (Beginner)

Duration: 10 minutes | Equipment: Agility ladder (or cones/chalk lines)

  1. Lay out an agility ladder flat on the ground, or mark 8-10 evenly spaced lines with chalk.
  2. Perform two-feet-in-each-box, then side shuffles, then in-in-out-out steps.
  3. Complete each pattern 3 times, resting 30 seconds between sets.
  4. Finish with a 10-metre sprint straight out of the ladder to connect footwork to game speed.

Tip: Stay on the balls of your feet and keep your steps light and quick rather than trying to go fast with heavy footfalls.
